How It Works

Get your team supported in three steps

No complex integrations. No lengthy procurement. Launch a world-class wellbeing programme for your workforce in days, not months.

1

Set up your programme

Tell us about your organisation and wellbeing goals. We'll design a tailored programme with the right therapist network and session allowances.

2

Invite your employees

Employees receive a private link to sign up. They browse therapists, book sessions, and access resources — all completely anonymously.

3

Track impact & ROI

Your admin dashboard shows anonymous, aggregated insights — engagement rates, utilisation trends, and wellbeing scores. No individual data exposed.
