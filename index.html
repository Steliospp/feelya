Get your team started in three simple steps

Set up your plan

Choose a plan that fits your organisation, configure your preferences, and onboard your team in minutes. Our team handles the heavy lifting so you don't have to.

Why Feelya

Usage Analytics

Flexible Plans

Scale up or down as your organisation grows. Whether you have 10 employees or 10,000, our plans adapt to your needs with no long-term lock-in contracts.

Video & Audio Sessions

Employees connect via secure video or audio — whichever they prefer. Our platform works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile. No downloads required.
