Why Feelya

Everything your workforce needs — nothing they don't

A complete employee mental health platform built for modern organisations.

Enterprise-Grade Security

SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, end-to-end encrypted sessions. Employee data is fully anonymised — you only see aggregated insights, never individual usage.

Accredited Therapists Only

Every therapist is UK-based and registered with the HCPC, BACP, UKCP, or BPS. Carefully vetted so your employees get the highest standard of care.

Real-Time Analytics

Track engagement rates, session utilisation, wellbeing trends, and programme ROI through your dedicated admin dashboard. Anonymous by design.

Effortless Onboarding

Share a single invite link. Employees sign up privately in under 60 seconds. No IT integration needed, no complex rollouts. Works alongside your existing benefits.

Wide Range of Specialities

Anxiety, depression, burnout, stress, relationships, trauma, grief — your employees get matched with therapists who specialise in exactly what they need.

Video & Audio Sessions

Employees connect via secure video or audio — whichever they prefer. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. No downloads, no plugins, no friction.
