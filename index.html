Why Feelya

Built around what matters most — you

We believe therapy should be accessible, private, and completely on your terms.

Private & Secure

End-to-end encrypted sessions with full privacy controls. You decide whether your therapist can see or hear you — your comfort, your rules.

Accredited Therapists

Every therapist is UK-based, highly qualified, and registered with the HCPC, BACP, UKCP, or BPS. Carefully vetted so you can focus on getting better.

On Your Schedule

Book sessions at times that suit your life — mornings, evenings, or weekends. No waiting rooms, no commute. Therapy that adapts to you.

No Membership Fees

Wide Range of Specialities

Video & Audio Sessions
